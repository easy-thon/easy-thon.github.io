# Key sounds

The keyboard sounds in the intro are real keyboards, recorded. Each pack here (`<id>.js`) holds single strokes cut
from one recording: keys going down, coming back up, and (where the recording has them) the space bar and enter. The
strokes were found by their onsets, trimmed, the room noise reduced where noted, and levelled, then laid end to end
in one FLAC file written into the script. `assets/js/stage/sound.js` loads and plays them.

Each pack keeps the licence of its recording.
